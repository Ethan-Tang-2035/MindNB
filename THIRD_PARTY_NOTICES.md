`npm run build:desktop` collects full top-level license and notice files from installed dependencies (including transitive and build dependencies) into `dist-desktop/THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt`. Installers include this file and this document in `resources/licenses/`. Electron's own notices remain alongside its runtime. Missing upstream notices are explicitly marked and require review; this inventory is not a legal certification.

The regression fixture documents contain meal-planning examples and embedded artwork. Review their source rights and screenshots before the first public source release. Private research, journals and generated drafts outside the fixture directory are excluded by the public-source manifest.

## Dependency supplements

Complete dependency notices are generated during desktop builds. Exact-version source files and declared SPDX license terms are recorded in [docs/licenses](docs/licenses/README.md), with origin URLs and checksums. Unknown declarations stop the build. Synthetic regression artwork and text are generated by `scripts/build-test-fixtures.py`; original personal sample documents are not distributed.
